ChaosMaker — Frontend UI Fix Plan
=================================
Source: full scan of all 24 QML files (~6,100 lines), 2026-11-06.
Rules: follow AGENTS.md — one step at a time, test only what changed, no regressions,
keep the 3-tier split (backend = pure C++, wrapper = Qt bridge, frontend = QML view only).
