Created 2026-11-07, when docs/frontend-fix-plan.txt was finished (all items done; 3.11 was
replaced by 6.2 Chaos EQ). This file collects everything still open, from that plan, from
docs/follow-trail.md, from docs/roadmap.txt and from the plan 6.2/6.3 sessions.

no regressions, keep the 3-tier split (backend = pure C++, wrapper = Qt bridge, frontend = QML

Before starting an item, check the code: some notes below come from older lists and may lag.

G. LOCAL-ONLY MATERIAL (in .gitignore, never committed)
--------------------------------------------------------
local-reference/ holds: q3.jpg (EQ reference screenshot), sampleGuitarRaw.wav and
soloGuitarRaw.wav (dry guitar DIs), needGuiterStem.mp3 / metal.mp3 / rock.mp3 (references,
with -ref.wav conversions), "Modern Boutique 4x12" IRs (48 kHz), presets/ (tone-matched
GuitarSuite presets: djent chords, metal solo, rock chords, rock solo) and renders/
(fxrender output). build/fxrender renders a WAV through pedals; fxrender --analyze compares
tone balance and crest factor (do not use --analyze on IR files: its Hann window hides an
impulse at the file start; measure IRs with an unwindowed FFT instead).
