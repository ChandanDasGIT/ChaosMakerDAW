This is the architecture of ChaosMaker

The three tiers: frontend/ (QML) uses wrapper/ (Qt C++ classes registered for QML), and wrapper/
uses backend/ (C++20 with no Qt includes). Folder paths follow the UI: for example topBar/appSettings/
is in all three tiers, and every instrument has its own folder under instruments/ in all three.
Each tier also has a common/ folder.
Left out: build*/ (generated), 2bDel/ (working notes), .gitkeep placeholders.
